## Photorealistic Simulation

NVIDIA Isaac Sim uses **RTX** ray-tracing to create simulations that look like the real world. This is crucial for training vision-based AI.

## Isaac ROS

A collection of hardware-accelerated ROS 2 packages for:
*   **VSLAM**: Visual Simultaneous Localization and Mapping.
*   **Object Detection**: Finding objects in the scene.
*   **Navigation**: Planning paths around obstacles.
